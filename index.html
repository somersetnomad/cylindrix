How is this scored?

Clear k rings with one piece and you score T(k) × 100 × level. T(k) is the k-th triangular number, 1 + 2 + … + k, so each extra ring is worth more than the last:

11002300360041,00051,500

Example: 3 rings at level 2 is T(3) × 100 × 2 = 6 × 100 × 2 = 1,200. Clearing them one at a time would give only 3 × 200 = 600.

Drops add a little too: +1 per row for a soft drop (↓), +2 per row for a hard drop (Space). Level goes up every 10 rings.

How to play

Your first game

  1. Press ← or → to turn the drum until the dashed outline sits over a gap.
  2. Press ↑ to rotate the piece so it fits the gap, then Space to drop it.
  3. Keep the stack level all the way round. Gaps on the back still count, so turn the drum to check them.
  4. If a piece doesn't fit anywhere, press C to hold it for later.

Rules

  • The well is a drum of 12 columns, 30° apart. There are no walls: a ring clears only when all 12 cells are filled.
  • Your piece always sits at the front. Moving turns the drum. The back half shows faintly through the glass, and the red line marks the seam at column 0.
  • Pieces grow with you: 2 trominoes until ring 8, 7 tetrominoes until ring 30, then 18 pentominoes. A program generates every shape; each size has its own shuffled bag.
  • Hold keeps one piece aside. You can swap once per piece.
  • In Drift, the drum turns one column on a timer that speeds up each level. It turns right on odd levels and left on even ones, and carries your piece if it would collide.
  • Piece colours are spaced by the golden angle, 137.5° around the colour wheel, so no two neighbouring shapes look alike.
